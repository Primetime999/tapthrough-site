Managing your subscription

TapThrough Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. You can view, change, or cancel it any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap TapThrough to change your plan or cancel.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the current billing period. Packs are one-time purchases and don't renew.

Restoring purchases

Bought Pro or a pack and don't see it on a device? In TapThrough, open Settings (the gear icon, top-right) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Common questions

Why are the walkthroughs shown in light mode?

Each step is a real screenshot captured in light mode, and the guidance markers are positioned to match. The rest of the app follows your system appearance — only the step viewer stays light so the screenshots line up. This is intentional.

Are the walkthroughs free?

Many are. Every app in TapThrough leads with free walkthroughs. Topic packs and Pro unlock the rest — packs are a one-time purchase, and Pro unlocks everything plus cross-app workflows.

Is TapThrough affiliated with the apps it covers?

No. TapThrough is an independent guide. App names and logos belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the app a walkthrough covers.

What does TapThrough collect about me?

Minimal, anonymous, aggregate analytics — never sold or shared with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
