Why TapThrough exists

You shouldn't need twenty minutes to figure out how to back up your photos, disable Gmail notifications, or set up Screen Time for your kid's iPhone. But Apple changes iOS every year. Apps redesign their menus every few months. The blog post that ranks first on Google was written for iOS 16 and the screenshots no longer match.

So you scroll, you guess, you give up — or you call your nephew.

TapThrough fixes that. Every walkthrough is captured on a real iPhone running the current iOS, recaptured within days of each Apple release, and shows you the exact button to tap on the exact screen you're looking at.

How it works

  1. Pick a task. “Turn off notifications,” “Delete history in Google Maps,” “Set up Apple Pay.”
  2. Swipe through it on your phone. One step per screen, with a red circle around the exact button to tap.
  3. Done. Most tasks take under a minute.

What's in the app

  • 100+ walkthroughs at launch, covering iPhone Settings, iCloud, Photos, Mail, and the most-used third-party apps.
  • Captured on the current iOS, with a hard rule against publishing any walkthrough on an obsolete version.
  • Recaptured within days of every Apple release, so the screenshots never go stale.
  • Topic packs for the high-leverage stuff: protect your privacy, set up a kid's first iPhone, fix battery drain.
  • Works offline. Walkthroughs ship with the app; you don't need a connection to read them.

We don't collect data about you. Ever.

TapThrough ships with zero analytics SDKs. No Google Analytics. No Firebase. No Mixpanel. No crash reporting service. No advertising IDs.

We don't know what walkthroughs you read, what time you opened the app, or what device you're on. We can't — we didn't build the wiring. The only personal data Apple shares with us is whether your in-app purchase succeeded, which Apple needs to do so we can unlock the content you paid for.
